Crossing out: the sieve

Crossing out, from 2 to two million
Dividing each number by everything up to its square root

Press Space for each step: 2, 3, 5 and 7 cross out their multiples, then the survivors light up. The last step races both methods below two million, live on this laptop. ← steps back; R replays the current step.

Below two million: 4,347,776 cross-outs against 179,492,731 divisions, about 41× less work. In Python it's 0.3 s against 19 s.

The point for a general audience: we found primes without ever dividing. Doing the work in a smarter order beats doing it faster.
